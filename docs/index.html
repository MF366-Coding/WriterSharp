WriterSharp is a code editor designed to be lightweight, fast, and intuitive, giving you the power to focus entirely on your coding without distractions.

With a modern interface and thoughtful features, every keystroke counts.

Why use WriterSharp?

Open-Source

One huge problem code editors sometimes carry is that they're not open-source and, even if they are, they're usually backed by a big corporate, causing you to be at the mercy of said corporate's decision. Unlike most code editors out there, WriterSharp is fully open-source and licensed under the permissive MIT License. Everyone can inspect the code, contribute improvements, or adapt it for their own workflows. Transparency and community-driven development are at the heart of WriterSharp.

Privacy-focused

WriterSharp is fully telemetry-free*, meaning your data never leaves your device. Write, debug, and explore features without worrying about hidden tracking or corporate surveillance. Your privacy is always protected.

*Some third-party plugins may collect data. WriterSharp cannot control this, so please exercise caution when installing extensions.

Cross-platform

WriterSharp runs smoothly on Windows, Linux, and MacOS, providing a consistent experience regardless of your system. Whether you're at home, on a laptop, or a development workstation, WriterSharp adapts seamlessly.

*WriterSharp on MacOS is currently untested.

Extensible

WriterSharp's plugin API allows you to extend and customize your environment. Add new features, tweak the interface, or integrate external tools. The API is designed to be future-proof, with flexibility and performance in mind, empowering power users to create a truly personalized experience.

AnaSense Integration

WriterSharp has a native integration with AnaSense, an upcoming technology made by me to analyze buffers in real-time. This enables smarter data processing and deeper insight into your projects.

AnaSense is, like WriterSharp, fully open-source and privacy-focused, ensuring high performance without compromising user trust or security.

Debugger

WriterSharp includes a simple yet robust debugging experience. You can set static line breakpoints, dynamic triggers for exceptions, inspect variables, evaluate expressions, and monitor runtime behavior. It's designed to make debugging code efficient, even for complex projects.
